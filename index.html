What this is

A set of short how-to guides for the ECB team, covering the tools and routines that keep the company running day to day. Each one is self-contained: open the one you need, when you need it.

You don't have to read them in order, and you don't have to read them all. Most people only ever need the first group.

New here? Start with the General guide and the Slack guide — they cover the two things you'll use every day.
