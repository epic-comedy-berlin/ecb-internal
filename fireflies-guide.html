Using Fireflies
in meetings

How to make sure every ECB meeting gets captured — whether you set it up, someone else did, or the call runs long.

What Fireflies does: A bot joins your Google Meet call, records everything, and produces a full transcript automatically. That transcript feeds into the Claude AI system so meeting decisions and tasks don't get lost.

Why this matters

The whole AI system depends on Fireflies capturing meetings. If it isn't in the call, there's no transcript — and without a transcript, Claude can't produce meeting reports, update the task files, or keep track of decisions.

Fireflies is connected to the ECB account at info@epiccomedyberlin.com. When a meeting is created from that Google Calendar, it joins automatically. But there are situations where it won't happen on its own — this guide covers all of them.

How to confirm Fireflies is recording

Look at the participant list in Google Meet. If Fireflies is recording, you'll see Fireflies.ai Notetaker listed as a participant. If it's not there, it's not recording — act immediately using the relevant steps below.

Situation 1 — ECB internal meeting

This is the normal case for Monday standups and Thursday work sessions. Fireflies joins automatically as long as the meeting was created from the info@epiccomedyberlin.com Google Calendar and you join as that account.

If you join late — check immediately

Fireflies waits in the Google Meet waiting room for a limited time. If nobody admits it before it times out, it leaves without recording anything.

If you join a meeting already in progress and Fireflies isn't visible in the participant list, assume it timed out. Go straight to the Add to Live Meeting steps in Situation 2. Don't wait to check after the meeting — by then it's too late.

How to join and get Fireflies recording

1

Open Google Calendar as info@epiccomedyberlin.com

Go to calendar.google.com. In the top-right corner, check which account you're viewing as. If it shows your personal account, click the icon and switch to info@epiccomedyberlin.com.

2

Join the meeting from there

Click the calendar event and select Join with Google Meet. On the pre-join screen, confirm the account shown in the top-right says info@epiccomedyberlin.com. If not, click Switch account before entering.

3

Admit Fireflies from the waiting room

Fireflies will appear in the waiting room as Fireflies.ai Notetaker. Click Admit. If you see a "potential risk" warning, click the three dots (⋮) next to it and select Admit entry — this is a standard Google Meet security prompt, not an actual problem.

Once admitted, Fireflies records silently for the rest of the call. You don't need to do anything else.

Situation 2 — External or third-party meeting

Any meeting not created from the ECB Google Calendar — a call with a venue, a partner, a contractor. Fireflies won't auto-join because it isn't on the info@ calendar.

Easiest option — ask the host to invite info@

If you know about the meeting in advance, ask the organiser to add info@epiccomedyberlin.com as a guest on the calendar invite. Fireflies will detect it and join automatically — no manual steps needed.

If it's last-minute or you can't ask the host, use the manual steps below.

If you join late — same rule applies

If Fireflies was invited but nobody admitted it in time, it will have timed out. Check the participant list as soon as you join. If Fireflies isn't there, add it manually using the steps below straight away.

How to add Fireflies manually

1

Open app.fireflies.ai in a new tab

Keep your Google Meet open. In a new browser tab, go to app.fireflies.ai and sign in as info@epiccomedyberlin.com.

2

Click the Capture button — top right

In the top-right corner of the dashboard, click the dropdown arrow (▾) next to the purple Capture button.

3

Select "Add to Live Meeting" and paste the link

Choose Add to Live Meeting from the dropdown. In the panel that appears, optionally enter a meeting name, paste the Google Meet link, and click Start capturing.

Fireflies will join the call within a few seconds and appear in the participant list.

4

Admit Fireflies — or ask the host to

Fireflies will appear in the waiting room. If you're the host, click Admit. If you're not the host, give them a heads-up so they can admit it — on Google Meet, only the host can admit participants.

Do this before the meeting starts when you can

It's easier to admit Fireflies from the waiting room before conversation starts than to interrupt mid-meeting. If you have the link in advance, add it early.

Situation 3 — Meeting running over 2 hours

The ECB Fireflies account is on the Pro plan. Fireflies can stay in a meeting for up to 2 hours before it leaves automatically. This is most likely to happen on long Thursday work sessions.

Watch for the 2-minute warning

Before it leaves, Fireflies posts a message in the Google Meet chat approximately 2 minutes before the time limit. When you see that message, wrap up or pause whatever is being discussed — use the 2 minutes to reach a natural stopping point before the handover.

How to hand over to a new Fireflies session

1

Reach a stopping point within 2 minutes

When you see the warning in the chat, finish the current point or pause mid-topic. A clean break in the conversation makes it easier to piece the two transcripts together later.

2

Remove Fireflies or wait for it to leave

Either wait for Fireflies to drop out automatically, or remove it manually from the participant list to end the first session cleanly. Both work — removing it yourself gives you more control over the timing.

3

Re-add Fireflies via Add to Live Meeting

Open a new browser tab, go to app.fireflies.ai, click Capture ▾ in the top right, select Add to Live Meeting, paste the meeting link, and click Start capturing. Fireflies will rejoin within a few seconds.

4

Admit Fireflies and announce the new session

Admit Fireflies from the waiting room. Once it's in, say out loud in the call something like: "This is Part 2 of today's meeting, continuing from Part 1." This gives both Fireflies and Claude a clear marker that the two transcripts belong together as one session.

What happens to the transcript

Fireflies saves the session as two separate entries in the Notebook:

Part What it contains For MIR runs
Part 1 Start of meeting up to the 2-hour mark Fetch both parts — Claude combines them into one session
Part 2 Everything after Fireflies rejoined Mention that the meeting ran over 2 hours when triggering the MIR

If something goes wrong

Fireflies didn't join an ECB meeting

Most likely cause: the meeting was joined from a personal Google account rather than info@epiccomedyberlin.com. If the meeting is still ongoing, go to app.fireflies.ai and use Add to Live Meeting immediately.

Fireflies appeared in the waiting room but nobody admitted it

It will have timed out and left. If the meeting is still running, use Add to Live Meeting to re-invite it. It will appear in the waiting room again.

No transcript after the meeting

Fireflies takes 5–10 minutes to process after a call ends. Check the Meetings section of the dashboard — "In Progress" means it's still processing. If nothing has appeared after 30 minutes, flag it in #p1-important.

Speakers are showing as Speaker 1, Speaker 2

This happens when participants join from unrecognised accounts. It doesn't affect the transcript content — names just need to be read in context. The Claude MIR process accounts for this automatically.
