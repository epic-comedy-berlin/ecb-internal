for everyone.

We share one Max plan. What you do affects what everyone else can do. This guide covers two habits that keep the account working for the whole team.

Read the General Guide first if you haven't — it covers models, token costs, and when to switch. Come back here for the two specific things: connectors and the usage window.

Turn off connectors you don't need

Active connectors load their tool definitions into every single message — whether you call them or not. Fireflies active on a briefing chat? You're paying for it on every reply, even though you never touched a transcript. Turn off anything that isn't relevant to the task you're doing right now.

Click the + button

Bottom-left of the chat input area in ECB Company Work.

Click Connectors

The connector panel opens on the right showing Fireflies, MailerLite, and Zapier.

Toggle off what you don't need

Blue = active (costs tokens on every message). Grey = off. Takes effect on your next message. Toggle back on whenever a task actually needs it.

Which connectors to keep on

Default for all three: off. Turn on only for the specific task that needs it.

Connector Turn on when... Leave off for...
Fireflies Pulling meeting transcripts — MIRs, task scans, LOG updates Briefings, writing, research, everything else
Zapier Building or debugging automations Briefings, copy tasks, newsletter, strategy
MailerLite Working directly with the email list — segments, automation setup Anything not specifically about MailerLite

Check the 5-hour window first

The account budget resets every five hours from your first message — not at midnight or any fixed time of day. If you start at 2pm, the reset might not come until 7pm. Check before starting anything long or Opus-heavy.

How to find the usage screen

Click your account name in the bottom-left corner of Claude, then go to Settings.

1

Click your account name (bottom-left) → Settings

Inside Settings, click Usage in the left sidebar.

2

Settings → Usage — current session bar shows how much of the 5-hour window is used and when it resets

Account name (bottom-left) → Settings → Usage

The Current session bar shows how much of the 5-hour budget is used and how long until it resets. If the bar is nearly full, wait for the reset or post in #p1-important so the rest of the team knows.

Standing rule — Aaron, 4 May

Nobody uses Claude in the three hours before a scheduled team meeting. Meetings are when we most need the account unblocked. Finish heavy work before the window starts.

Flag heavy work before you start

If your task is time-sensitive and will use a lot of Opus, post in #p1-important first. Not asking permission — just making sure nobody else fires a big session at the same time and tips the account over the limit mid-task.

#p1-importantStarting contract review now — Opus, ~1 hour. Hold off on heavy work until I'm done.

If the limit hits

·

Post in #p1-important immediately — don't let others keep trying into a locked account
